A little about me

Usually making something.

I'm Zahraa, a student from Lebanon. I like working with cardboard, paper, and wood - especially when I can turn them into a tiny building or something useful for my room. This is a place to keep the things I’ve made as I learn.

A few favorite materials: paper, cardboard & wood.
